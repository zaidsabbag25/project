# Local demonstration artwork

All files in `public/images/` are original vector illustrations created for this project during the React rebuild. They are local development placeholders, not photographs or representations of actual products available for sale.

No third-party photographs, remote image hotlinks, external fonts, or paid assets are required. The project owner may use, modify, and replace these original illustrations with the application.

Each SVG includes an accessible title and description. Product illustrations use an 800 × 900 view box, and the room scene uses 1600 × 1000. All artwork is resolution independent. Replace placeholders with authorized product photography before presenting real inventory to customers.

## Product-gallery detail views

`chair-detail.svg`, `sofa-detail.svg`, `table-detail.svg`, `lamp-detail.svg`, `bed-detail.svg`, and `vase-detail.svg` are locally contained close-up crops of the corresponding original illustrations. Each uses a tighter 400 × 450 view box, retains the same palette and portrait aspect ratio, and has a distinct accessible title describing the close-up. These provide a second gallery view without external dependencies; they are demonstration illustrations, not additional photographs or claims about real material specifications.
